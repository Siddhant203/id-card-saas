Common PVC Printing Mistakes

PVC card printing requires careful alignment and proper settings to achieve professional results.

Using Low Resolution Files

Low-quality images can produce blurry and unclear printed cards.

Incorrect Scaling

Automatic page scaling may distort card dimensions during printing.

Poor Alignment

Improper card alignment can lead to cutting errors and uneven margins.

Skipping Test Prints

Printing without testing may waste PVC sheets and printer ink.

Ignoring Printer Settings

Incorrect printer settings may affect color quality and sharpness.
